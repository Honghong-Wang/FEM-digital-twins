## Interpretation Note

The sparse-observation assimilation tables should be presented as digital-twin calibration and failure-analysis tables. The posterior is a multi-start inversion proxy, not a full Bayesian sampler. The calibrated table improves empirical coverage through posterior spread inflation, so the manuscript should report coverage together with NLL, ECE, interval width, sharpness, and spread scale rather than claiming a fully Bayesian posterior.
